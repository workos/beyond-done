## Before you begin

This is a starter lesson. Replace it with your class introduction, prerequisites, and setup instructions.

Keep a notes file open as you work. Write down what you tried, what happened, and what you still need to check.

## What you will practice

- Give a model a clear task and useful context.
- Compare its output with evidence.
- Decide what to keep, change, or reject.

If a word is unfamiliar, look it up in the [glossary](/glossary/).

## Exercise: choose a task

Pick a small task you can finish during the class. Write down:

1. The result you want.
2. The information needed to produce it.
3. A check that would tell you whether it worked.

> Keep the task small enough that you can check the result yourself.
